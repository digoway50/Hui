Wholesale Production Policies & Terms

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Standard catalog MOQ is 50 pieces per style/color. We accommodate standard carton split ratios (e.g. 1:2:2:1 for S:M:L:XL). Custom Pantone lab dip dyeing requires 150 pieces per colorway.

How does Sample Ordering work?

Individual fit and fabric sample pieces are dispatched via DHL Express in 5–7 business days (Rs. 7,000–Rs. 14,000 per unit depending on silhouette). 100% of sample fees are credited toward your subsequent bulk run.

Private Labeling & Custom Packaging

Every piece can be delivered unbranded blank or customized with high-definition woven neck labels, organic care tags, and custom printed frosted polybags featuring your brand logo.

Production Timelines & Shipping Methods

Stock blanks dispatch in 3–5 business days. Custom labeled/printed production runs require 12–15 business days. We provide global DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) air freight (4–6 days) and ocean container shipping.
